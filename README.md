The scariest horror story I could write in exactly two sentences (205 characters).

`main.ts` writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`, validating the gate first: 1–400 characters, exactly two sentences.

> After my son's funeral, I kept his bedroom exactly as he left it, down to the glass of water on the nightstand. This morning the glass was empty, and there were wet footprints leading from his bed to mine.
